# Natural-language demo dataset

Use `nlp_investigation_demo.txt` in **Add your own data** or upload it as a text file. It is synthetic and contains no real personal information.

It is designed to demonstrate:

- Person, organization, vehicle, phone, account, and location extraction
- Calls, emails, payments, employment, visits, vehicle use, ownership, family, and association relationships
- INR amounts, payment references, dates, and source sentence retention
- Transcript-style speaker lines and narrative paragraphs
- Uncertain statements, witness attribution, and unresolved pronouns
- AI briefing after the extracted relationships are accepted
- Blockchain evidence sealing after the narrative becomes an investigation

For a clear presentation, explain that the system extracts candidate relationships from language, preserves the original text, and asks the investigator to review uncertain links. It does not decide whether an allegation is true.
